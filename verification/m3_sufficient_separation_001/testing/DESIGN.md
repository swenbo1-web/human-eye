## Timing boundaries

There are exactly six pair inputs and five alternating reference/candidate repetitions per input, giving sixty calls. The first four inputs are the same G508 three pairs and simple control. The final two are frozen intersecting bars and complete containment. Reference source is the original fac9 private baseline, with only the three previously disclosed quantity overrides. Its AABB early out, leaf arithmetic, exact-bit limits and deadline remain fixed.

Source loading is measured once at batch level. Each call then measures fresh input allocation, fresh query/options/holder preparation, solving and full semantic result serialization. The complete-business clock starts at input preparation and ends after serialization. Its thirty-second deadline starts at the same earliest point and is never renewed for support or fallback. Internal integer-frame and tree work remains inside solving. No separately unmeasured internal preparation cost is reported as its own window.

Primary timing uses actual product counters and the original deadline callback. Additional function spies and per-checkpoint dictionaries are absent. Independent correctness checks run on saved results outside these windows. One fixed six-case candidate-only non-timing pass can measure actual calls and work after root authorization, separately from the sixty samples. No cross-call memo, replacement or selective retry is allowed.

The G508 gate compares the sum of complete-business medians and requires at least twenty percent improvement with no individual pair regression. Control and fallback allowances are frozen in FROZEN.json. Reference and candidate solve the common business relation/distance problem with different additional shell guarantees, which must remain disclosed. An incomplete reference or candidate cannot supply a successful cost comparison.
