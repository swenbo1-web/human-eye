## Remaining bottleneck and scope

The remaining bottleneck is insufficient multi-pair coverage per node certificate in the fixed candidate tree. Peer certifies 2,934 descendant pairs in 2,775 certificates, about 1.06 covered pairs per certificate. Competing and Near each cover 3,200 pairs in 3,013 certificates, also about 1.06. Most successful certificates therefore exclude single pairs or small groups. Their preparation, node and common-anchor work still consumes enough of the fixed budget to prevent completion. Plane projections and the original traversal remain substantial alongside that work.

The fixed spatial tree may distribute triangles sharing a useful fan anchor across different nodes. Mixed projected orientations and degenerate two-coordinate side faces may also prevent higher-node metadata. Those explanations are plausible from the construction. This run did not measure their separate contribution or test an alternative grouping. It supplies no basis to select another axis, tree strategy, threshold or budget.

The batch therefore retains the three closed unknown results and closes this algorithm attempt as partial/failed against its completion condition. Source binding is corrected and the simple saved control remains valid. Independent mechanism regressions, the root-coordinated finite kernel timing, isolated installation and actual MCP checks remain separate acceptance evidence. They must retain this closed failure even if their other assertions pass. No additional algorithm optimization or G508 count rerun is authorized in this batch.
