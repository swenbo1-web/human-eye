## 1. Conservative bound and strict cutoff

For integer axis n and a closed integer box B=[lo,hi], exact support extrema are

`l(B)=sum(n[k]*(lo[k] if n[k]>=0 else hi[k]))`

`u(B)=sum(n[k]*(hi[k] if n[k]>=0 else lo[k]))`.

Every point in B, including degenerate faces/edges/points and its boundary, has projection in `[l(B),u(B)]`. If two existing children partition every native triangle row of internal node S and each child box covers all its referenced vertices, use `[min(l(child0),l(child1)), max(u(child0),u(child1))]`. This interval covers the union of their triangles and its convex hull. It need not equal the interval of their parent AABB: different support corners of that parent can be invented combinations absent from either child. A leaf uses its existing leaf box. A missing child, invalid bounds, uncertain coverage or different integer frame yields no new proof. The box partition/membership comes from the original BVH construction, not from matching bounds or names.

The new envelope is a subset of the parent-box projection interval. It never excludes a member. For a pure single-axis direction, its endpoints equal the parent interval because the parent already has the same coordinate extrema; no incremental direction information exists. The contract's skip is justified. At least one internal side is required to distinguish this mechanism from an existing leaf-box check. Even a tighter interval may add no exclusion beyond original Euclidean AABB or the downstream original032 plane/leaf tests; those are separate increment denominators.

For left/right intervals, let `g=max(lA-uB,lB-uA,0)`. Cauchy–Schwarz gives every pair of covered members squared Euclidean distance at least `g*g/(n·n)`. With current exact incumbent `N/D`, only `g*g*D>(n·n)*N` excludes. Since g is a nonnegative integer, this is exactly `g > isqrt(((n·n)*N)//D)`, including noninteger quotients. Equality cannot exclude a later lower native-row witness. Use the actual current032 projection state's integer cutoff, axis, exact incumbent and epoch; no new axis or floating normalization, no queue shift and no stale context. The state must still belong to the current best, original direction eligibility and current pair's integer frame. Best zero is outside the contract's optional direction eligibility.

The tiny independent fixture gives actual extra information: child points `(0,0,0)` and `(2,2,0)`, right leaf point `(3,1,0)`, axis `(1,-1,0)`, incumbent squared `3/2`. Child envelope `[0,0]` versus parent `[-2,2]`, right projection2. Parent projection does not exclude; Euclidean parent AABB lower squared1 does not exclude; child gap2 exceeds cutoff1 and is conservative (actual minimum squared2). At incumbent squared2 the exact equality is retained. This fixture proves interval arithmetic, not real workload value.

## 2. A sharper limit on removable original work

For any two AABBs and any nonzero direction, their directional interval gap divided by the direction norm is no greater than the exact Euclidean distance between the boxes. To prove this, choose an exact closest pair of box points (independent coordinate clamps attain it); their projection difference bounds the interval gap from above, and Cauchy–Schwarz bounds that difference by norm times their Euclidean distance. Thus `gap² <= (n·n)*AABB_distance²`.

Every descendant leaf-node box is contained in an immediate child box of the tested internal side; a tested leaf side is already its own covering box. Once the child-envelope proof strictly excludes the node, all eventual descendant leaf-node projection intervals are also strictly separated. Their original exact Euclidean node AABB tests must therefore exclude them before original leaf-pair iteration. The original best can only decrease, so later incumbents cannot invalidate that implication. Queue shifts do not weaken the original exact exclusion test. The original run may terminate unknown before reaching them, but cannot perform an in-domain narrow call on a descendant that satisfies these premises.

The mechanism's direct savings are consequently limited to original queue construction/maintenance, node visits and bound tests before those existing rejections. It does **not** directly remove original leaf-pair visits, plane tests,032 leaf projection proofs or exact narrow calls inside the excluded native domain. A nonzero saved-narrow count in a shadow report would indicate a membership, frame, incumbent, source-event or state-association error. It may avoid many nodes, but neither their covered triangle-pair product nor whole-tree time is saved exact work. This tighter theorem can reduce the optimistic saving bound without running a prototype. The separate future-credit effect below can still change narrow work outside that domain.

## 3. Finite arithmetic and cost

The existing primitive axis has at most128 magnitude bits per component, and its construction already checked512-bit local scratch, norm and cutoff. Original frame coordinates can require4096 bits. Therefore optional child-box support is not universally512-bit: a coordinate×axis product could need4224 bits before summation. Original finite binary64 inputs do not imply the optional512 limit is always satisfied. Precheck every multiply and signed add under the existing local cap before executing it. A refusal is local no-proof fallback to original032; never convert an optional width refusal into a new geometry unknown, and never catch original deadline/budget/abort exceptions as local fallback. Gap subtraction can require one more bit than an endpoint and needs its own precheck. Reusing the existing cutoff avoids a new gap square/cross-product and avoids a new axis cost.

For two internal sides there are four child boxes, two support endpoints each: at most8 three-dimensional dots, hence24 component products and24 accumulated additions in the straightforward guarded form. There are at most24 support-coordinate choices,4 child-interval min/max comparisons,2 gap subtractions,2 max comparisons and1 cutoff comparison, plus constant eligibility, binding and budget checks. One internal/one leaf case has6 dots; the permitted mechanism has no descendant scan, recursion, index maintenance or retained cache. Temporary scalars/tuples are bounded independently of subtree face count. This is a finite logical-operation bound, not yet a frozen `_Work` charge. Source-specific accounting must also price all choices, checks, merges, failure prefixes and bookkeeping. Do not present48 arithmetic operations alone as the total cost, and do not treat telemetry clocks or original skipped callbacks as free product work.

Near the original work cap, local remaining headroom must cover the entire admitted optional attempt, its failure path, state cleanup and the original path's necessary publication/checkpoint margin. Admission cannot allocate an optimistic mean cost. New checks must remain cooperatively interruptible by the original callback and preserve its original deadline; no renewed timeout. Full work, narrow, bit and4096-face limits remain unchanged. A finite local operation bound does not itself prove the original032 query remains known.

## 4. Original032 credit is nonseparable

Each `_nearest_tree` starts a new zero-balance `_ProjectionCredit`. At shift0 the original dequeue AABB checkpoint saves one work unit and earns one credit; shift>0 does not. Existing leaf attempts must fit their own settled available credit and remaining original work, debit actual cost even on failure, and only then earn the proved narrow floor on strict success. These rules may not be weakened or shared across pairs/queries.

An early internal prune removes descendants' original dequeue credits. The theorem above excludes direct old leaf-prune earnings inside this particular proved domain; do not invent them. Missing dequeue credits can nevertheless disable a later old leaf attempt outside the pruned subtree. That later pair can then enter exact narrow phase, change subsequent successful-axis availability, consume more units and potentially reach unknown. Incumbent/witness invariance does not imply credit-path or cost invariance.

Consequently, neither `new_attempt_cost<=current032balance` nor `new_attempt_cost<=remaining_work` proves dominance relative to original032. Using the same credit account can itself suppress a later original admission. A stronger abstract example optimistically makes the new check free. Omitting six descendant nodes saves their18 push/queue-key/visit units but loses their six shift0 saved-bound credits. A later fully warm original032 projection needs its exact five-unit admission, which the original six-credit path affords and the zero-credit altered path cannot. Original cost over those events is18+5=23; altered later narrow work has a35-unit normal-triangle floor, already twelve units higher before any real new-proof cost. An existing unchanged axis and warm intervals can precede this comparison; no in-domain exact work is needed. This is an operation/ledger counterexample, not an assertion of a particular native geometry or measured gain.

To authorize stage2, a real source-specific proof must compare with the actual original032 path, include disappearing downstream credits/proofs and lost savings, and preserve original known/unknown behavior near the cap. An aligned prefix argument must identify corresponding original events, count only work genuinely already removed after paying all replacement effects, and preserve pending/finally debit invariants. Alternatively a conservative remaining-original-work/publication bound must actually be established for the current state. Merely pointing to031/032's older proof against its pre-credit baseline is insufficient: the comparator here is032 itself. No such production dominance proof is supplied by this document. Until it exists, take the original032 path and retain unknown. A stage1 shadow run can diagnose the gap but cannot substitute for this proof.

## 5. Shadow attribution and overlap

The unchanged A traversal is the source of truth in stage1. Log a hypothetical new exclusion only after the original AABB passed, under the actual current eligible axis/incumbent. An excluded node pair's native domain is its left-row set×right-row set. Descendant domains can be nested inside an earlier hypothetical exclusion. Summing their face-pair products, old calls or old durations double counts. Use a verified disjoint antichain or union of native/event identities for any aggregate, and report raw sites separately. Distinct geometric regions can still share host/preparation overhead that this mechanism does not remove.

Heap ordering interleaves descendants with unrelated node pairs. A parent's first/last descendant timestamps are not one removable continuous wall interval. Attribute only the union of actual same-clock downstream events or nonoverlapping original logical spans assigned to that domain; retain unrelated intervals and required verification/publication. Original AABB must exclude eventual leaf-node descendants under the proved premises. Credit only actual earlier node/queue operations, not imaginary narrow work or all covered pairs. Traversal time for the entire tree is never the gross saving of a few hypothetical exclusions.

Shadow decisions are not generally composable. This particular mechanism does not directly remove in-domain completed exact calls, but lost credits can cause extra exact work elsewhere, changing completed-exact counts, projection epochs and later eligible sites. A direction available in the unchanged A trajectory cannot automatically be assumed available in a jointly pruned trajectory. A purported additional exclusion following an earlier hypothetical one is therefore an isolated diagnostic, not a cumulative prediction, unless actual reachability and state equivalence are established. Lost epoch/direction or original credit effects must reduce the optimistic bound or remain explicit uncertainty. Interval proofs are geometrically sound in their recorded A state; a sum of their timings is not automatically a feasible algorithm.
