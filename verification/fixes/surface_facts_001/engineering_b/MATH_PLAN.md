# B exact AABB pruning plan

For every prepared integer triangle, retain its three coordinate minima and maxima. For a triangle pair, interval separation in each coordinate is `g[k]=max(a_min[k]-b_max[k], b_min[k]-a_max[k], 0)`. Every point pair on those triangles has squared integer-frame distance at least `L2=sum(g[k]^2)`. The bound applies before either intersection or positive-distance feature evaluation, with no assumptions about topology or triangle orientation.

An incumbent is exact `n/d` in those same integer-frame squared units. Both are nonnegative, d positive. Because L2 is integral, `L2 > n/d` is equivalent to `L2 > floor(n/d)`. Start `remaining=n//d`. At each axis, `gap > isqrt(remaining)` proves a strictly larger bound and permits immediate rejection. Otherwise the square is at most remaining, and subtracting it is exact. Exhausting all axes without exceeding remaining keeps the pair. This preserves equality and the original first witness and avoids a potentially large `L2*d` product. No float or world factor enters the comparison.

Coordinates and retained incumbent operands retain their original 4096-bit checks. An interval subtraction may transiently require at most 4097 bits, inside the existing 8193-bit scratch allowance. A square is formed only after proving it fits remaining, hence at most 4096 bits. remaining and its exact integer square root are at most 4096 bits. No budget is widened and no unrelated pair overflow is introduced by cross multiplication.

All preparation/nondegeneracy/topology validation remains before search. The original pair checkpoint is executed for every visited pair, including rejected pairs. No new checkpoint cadence, feature precomputation, traversal ordering, capture/cache architecture or numerical publication changes are permitted. triangle_pairs remains the count of visits; aabb_bound_tests and aabb_pruned_pairs are added, while all old narrow-stage counters mean actual execution. The METHOD and pair cache algorithm identity advance for B only.

Independent answers and the fixed 8-session experiment are pre-registered in PREREGISTRATION.json and the coordinator's hashed B assignment. The previous A installation remains untouched and supplies each A sample; the same original control asset, helper bytes and counting adapter are used for both versions. Only one product candidate and one fixed sample set are allowed. A failed quality, work-reduction or complete-query gate recommends retaining A.
