**SURFACE-FACTS-001 A mathematics and finite work plan**

The objects are the current captured binary64 world-coordinate triangle surfaces. Only vertices referenced by faces participate. The frame origin is the first referenced A vertex. Each coordinate is converted to its exact dyadic rational and subtracted from that origin before a shared power-of-two integer scale is formed.

Admission checks total A+B faces at most 4096 and cross-object triangle pairs at most 16384 before copying face or coordinate arrays. Indices, finite referenced coordinates and nondegenerate exact triangle normals are checked for all admitted faces before an intersecting pair can terminate the search. Triangle-edge adjacency is counted across all current faces. Boundary edges have incidence one and nonmanifold edges incidence above two. Duplicate faces and nonmanifold topology do not certify any solid relation.

Each admitted triangle pair first uses the existing exact triangle intersection mathematics, including both plane slices and coplanar clipping. An intersection yields a common original-surface point and an exact zero distance. Otherwise the candidates are six point-to-face interiors, eighteen point-to-edge segments including endpoints, and nine edge-to-edge interiors. The closest-feature theorem covers the positive triangle distance after intersection has been excluded.

Point-face squared distance is d*d / dot(n,n), with exact projected barycentric numerators. Point-edge squared distance is (dot(q,q)*L - t*t) / L for an interior parameter, and the exact endpoint squared distance otherwise. For two nonparallel edges, D=dot(cross(u,v),cross(u,v)), s=(ab*bv-bb*au)/D, t=(aa*bv-ab*au)/D. Interior-edge squared distance is dot(w,cross(u,v)) squared divided by D. All squared candidates are compared by integer numerator/denominator cross products. Only the winning feature descriptor is retained, and only its final rational points are constructed.

Stored exact integers and reduced rational numerators/denominators are bounded at 4096 bits. A single unreduced product, comparison cross product or rational addition may require at most 8193 scratch bits before reduction and the next operand check. Three small local checked predicates reuse the formulas of surface_geometry.intersection, plane_slice and clip_coplanar, leaving that module unchanged. This bounds retained arithmetic operands without excluding a representable 2^-600 gap solely because its square underflows binary64.

Every face, referenced-vertex conversion, triangle pair and feature candidate invokes the existing request checkpoint. The intersection adapter forwards its consume/check points to the same checkpoint. Cancellation/deadline exceptions propagate. No product matrix, BVH or triangle-AABB distance pruning is allocated. A records actual admitted face, pair, predicate and feature counts. B's proposed AABB lower-bound pruning remains a separate future opportunity.

The final distance uses exact integer square-root rounding to binary64. An exact bit-length comparison chooses the square-root exponent, then a power-of-two scaling produces binary64 significand units. The integer floor is found by isqrt(N//D), and the exact comparison 4N versus D*(2k+1)^2 implements nearest-even halfway rounding. This covers both normal and subnormal boundaries without converting the square to float. Scaling first cancels powers of two and remains inside the declared 8193-bit scratch bound. A positive result rounding to zero stays unknown with a specific range reason. The 80-digit Decimal square root is used only for direction normalization, independently of scalar classification. World points use exact world addition followed by binary64 conversion. Exact squared Euclidean displacement bounds certify each point and the change of the point-pair displacement within 1e-9 m. The final represented point-pair distance is also checked within 1e-9 m. A positive distance never publishes an identical rounded point pair. Known distance and direction survive a world-point publication refusal. Zero-distance direction is not applicable.

Independent answers come from the assignment's parallel-plane G construction, the edge-through-face intersection, exact analytic points/segments/planes, and existing analytic box/containment fixtures. Product SAT and the new kernel are not truth generators.
