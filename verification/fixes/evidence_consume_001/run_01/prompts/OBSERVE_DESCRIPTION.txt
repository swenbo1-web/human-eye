Read fresh Blender facts or images. For named/ID text facts use query.mode='task', objects, requested_facts and include_image=false. aabb accepts 1..4 objects; pair facts take two in A-to-B order. task-query-v3 pair_relation returns zero-tolerance separation with surface gap, original points and A-to-B direction; computed surface touching with zero gap and undefined direction; or penetration with certified escape evidence. Escape is a translation of A, not surface distance, nearest direction or minimum depth. Requested unknowns remain partial. binding.runtime_source_sha256 identifies code; scene_revision/fingerprint identify the current capture including source and revision. trace_id supports diagnostics. surface_proximity independently measures nonnegative triangle-surface distance, original points, positive-distance A-to-B direction and boundary/nonmanifold edge counts. Zero-distance direction is not applicable. Edge counts alone do not certify a solid. Goals evaluate caller criteria. Other observation modes retain images and returned object IDs; scene changes expire observations. mesh_structure uses registered single-target tasks; diagnose yields facts/patch references, patch requests images.
