Apply the same ten requirements to each sealed original final. Scope is Object_01 paired with Object_02, Object_03 and Object_04 in the initial asset. When an original final is absent, retain all ten entries as `unrun`. For a supplied final, grade `pass`, `fail` or `unverified` against the public wording and independently bound evidence. Use `fail` for a false, contradictory or omitted answer requirement and `unverified` when evidence needed to decide a claim is unavailable or invalid. Keep each arm's denominator at ten and record the exact answer excerpts supporting each decision. A reasoned uncertainty identifies the missing evidence, and the affected sought fact stays unresolved.

Input byte hashes and the certified initial values are frozen in `EXPECTED.json`. Use only the initial historical snapshot and its independent error certificate. The terminal check compares evaluated local vertex arrays, ordered oriented triangles and matrix values with that snapshot, reconstructs world metres from the transform and scale, and compares them with both the historical world vertices and fresh export. It checks source metadata, runtime copy bytes, two distinct copied paths, per-slot device receipts and unchanged-scene guards. The matching pair and verified geometry delimit what can be claimed for this particular asset and configuration. Retain actual per-arm evidence failures in the verdict. Evidence from the other arm cannot fill a slot's missing evidence.

For a printed number at decimal place `d` (including explicit scientific notation), the half-step is `0.5 × 10^(-d)` in metres. A gap passes when its rounding interval overlaps the historical interval and `abs(reported − analytic_reference) + certified_distance_error_bound ≤ 1e-6 m`. For coordinates, the Euclidean norm of three half-steps bounds endpoint rounding. Measure each endpoint against its complete checked evaluated surface and separately require the endpoint distance to fall in the certified interval expanded by the sum of endpoint rounding bounds. Independently project both endpoints to their checked surfaces and also bound the projected separation using the resulting endpoint displacements. Apply the historical world arithmetic allowance to independently reconstructed geometry comparisons. Verify an explicitly cited triangle, if supplied as geometric support, in its stated object-local domain or an equivalent resolvable domain.

The transverse check clips each intact triangle by the other triangle's plane, requires strict sidedness in both and a positive overlap of the clips along their intersection line. A reported localization must be consistent with that positive segment. The strict interior check requires both oriented closed shells to have unit absolute winding number at the point and positive clearances exceeding the claimed location's rounding uncertainty. Verify any additional proof quantity or geometric scope the answer relies on from the current arrays. The scorer has no model, Blender or product solver calls.
