# Same-query exact boundary witness reuse

The accepted closed-shell classifier already prepares both surfaces once. This candidate also retains the first actual nonempty cross-surface exact intersection, its original triangle indices, and the identity of that ordered prepared pair. It does not infer boundary contact from a solid relation: contained surfaces can have a positive gap.

The consumer verifies the current ordered mesh identities, exact pair identity, triangle references, and exact closed-triangle membership. Invalid contact metadata falls back to the existing nearest traversal; preparation for a different ordered pair is discarded. An accepted contact supplies the existing zero-distance contact descriptor, after which rounding, original-world witness publication, precision limits, rank keys, and undefined zero-distance direction follow the original path. Nothing private is added to results or persistent cache values.

The holder exists only when both facts are requested. A classifier cache hit leaves it empty. A surface cache hit ignores it. Standalone surface queries retain the old path. Actual contact may remain usable when solid classification is conservatively unknown. Cancellation is not converted into geometry unknown.

One candidate and one fresh paired A/B are authorized. Baseline b60dd327 / 1545da06 is referenced in its preceding sealed source directory; candidate bytes are frozen after targeted tests. Both arms use identical profiling and original assembly inputs. Acceptance requires all contracts, three real-witness nearest skips, at least 10% improvement in six cold/changed full observe intervals, and unchanged regression no greater than max(10 ms, 10%). No repeat or second candidate is permitted.
